Methods in brief

Floods: Sentinel-1 radar (VH below −18 dB, or both polarisations), checked against Sentinel-2 in 2022. Dry-season radar water is shown only to illustrate its errors.

Drought: SPI from CHIRPS v2.0 with gamma fits to 1991–2020, run theory for events, MODIS NDVI and TerraClimate as independent checks.

Future rainfall: 23 NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 models; GEV by L-moments per 0.25° cell; uplift is the 2041–2070 value divided by the model's own 1985–2014 value.

Full methods, limits and references are on the study page.

About this explorer

Every value here is read from the results files, which the study's scripts write. Nothing is typed in. Maps show cells as they were computed; read patterns, not single cells, for the design-rainfall layers, where each cell has a 30-year sample.
